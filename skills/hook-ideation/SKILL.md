# Hook and format ideation

Gives the user hook and format pairs they can shoot or cut. A table, not an essay. Works for any subject, niche or platform.

## What to feed it

Any of these. None is required.

| Input | Example |
|---|---|
| The subject | A topic, a product, a trip, a story, a half-formed idea |
| Footage or images | A clip, screenshots or photos to build around |
| Platform and length | "Instagram reel, 20 seconds" |
| Goal | More reach, followers, a sale, a video tease, a brand deal |
| Text style | Font, colour and placement for on-screen text, if the user has one |

## Adapt to the user

Everything comes from what the user gives you in this chat. Never fall back on a fixed niche, past posts or someone else's brand.

- If they attached a clip or photos, make one contact sheet and read that. Never read the raw video frame by frame.
- If platform or length is missing, assume a 9:16 vertical video under 30 seconds and say so in one line.
- If the goal is missing, infer it from what they said.
- Ask one short multiple-choice question at most, and only if the answer would change the ideas. If you can't ask, state your assumption and carry on.
- If they have saved templates, a content calendar or recent posts in a connected folder, check them first. Reuse a format that already works and don't repeat a recent post.

## Output

One table, 6 to 8 rows, best idea first.

| # | Hook (on-screen text, max 2 lines) | Format | First 2 seconds | Footage needed | Why it should work |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Hook:** final on-screen copy, ready to paste. Short lines, plain words.
- **Format:** a named structure from the bank below, or one the user already has.
- **First 2 seconds:** what the viewer sees and hears.
- **Footage needed:** the shots. Flag any the user may not have, so they can be asked for.
- **Why it should work:** one short line. Facts, no hype.
- Vary the ideas. Never give 8 versions of the same structure.
- Under the table, give the top pick a short shot list.

## Hook rules

- The hook lands inside 2 seconds and reads with the sound off.
- Lead with the payoff, the odd detail or the tension. Never the intro.
- One idea per post.
- Truthful. No invented claims, fake scarcity or staged moments presented as real.
- Respect how the user shoots. If they don't pan, track or use a tripod, don't suggest it. Use edit moves instead (cuts, zooms, speed ramps).
- Default on-screen text, unless the user gives their own style: 2 main lines, one colour, dark outline, centred, about 74% of frame width, nothing in the bottom 18% where apps put their buttons.

## Hook formulas

- The odd detail
- The number ("3 things...")
- The mistake ("Most people get this wrong")
- The reveal (hold the subject back and build first)
- The test ("Is X worth it?")
- The POV (what it looks like from inside)
- The before and after
- The ranking
- The question people keep asking
- The contrarian take

## Format bank

Format names from byjoeym's "35 S-Tier Ad Formats to Steal" (a partial list, 31 of the 35). Names only: work out how each one fits the subject.

Censored, The truth, Don't be an idiot, X for X, Song playlist, Tap your goals, Native, Let's talk numbers, Science article, Tier list, Venn diagram, Zero stars, Email screenshot, Doodle, Reddit style, Quiz, Cartoon, Recipes, WhatsApp text, X signs you, Breaking news, Sourcing, Avoid this, Whiteboard, Greenscreen, Receipt, Partnership carousels, Notification offer, Demographic callout, Text on skin, Stat headline, Free ticket.

If the user saves a new reference format, add it to the list for this chat and reuse it.

## After the table

Offer one next step only: a shot list for the top pick, a caption with hashtags, or saving the winning format as a template. Then stop and let the user react. Refine from their picks.
